$$C=\frac{Q}{V_{ab}}$$
$V_{ab}$ is potential difference between the plates
# Measured in Farads
$$1F = \frac{1C}{V}$$
Capacitance of a capacitor depends solely on structural factors, shape, area, separation, and insulating material

# Most common configuration is two parallel plates

Recall two charged plates like this has uniform [[Electric Fields]]
Potential difference between the plates is
$V_{ab}=Ed$

## Capacitance
$$C=\frac{Q}{V_{ab}}=\frac{Q}{Ed}=\frac{\epsilon_{0}A}{d}$$
Spherical
$$\vec{E}=\frac{Q}{4\pi \epsilon_{0}r^2}\hat{r}$$
$$V_{ab}=\frac{Q}{4\pi \epsilon_{0}}\left( \frac{1}{r_{a}} -\frac{1}{r_{b}} \right)$$
$$C=\frac{Q}{V_{ab}}=\frac{4\pi \epsilon_{0}}{\left( \frac{1}{r_{a}-\frac{1}{r_{b}}} \right)}$$
Cylander
$$\hat{E}=\frac{\lambda}{2\pi r \epsilon_{0}}\hat{r}=\frac{Q}{2\pi \epsilon_{0}rL}\hat{r}$$
$$C=\frac{2\pi \epsilon_{0}L}{\ln\left( \frac{r_{b}}{r_{a}} \right)}$$
